[H3] Implied Warranty of Habitability in California
The warranty of habitability in California is a legal doctrine that requires landlords to maintain rental properties in a habitable condition. This means ensuring that the property is safe, sanitary, and fit for human habitation. Landlords must provide basic amenities such as heating, plumbing, and electricity, and address any issues that affect the tenant's health or safety. Failure to meet these standards can result in legal action by the tenant, including rent withholding or termination of the lease. Our Pittsburg landlord attorney can help those on either side of the legal battle navigate California landlord tenant laws.
