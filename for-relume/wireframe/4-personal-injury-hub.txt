[HEADER] Understanding Your Case
--------------------------------------------------
[H2] The 4 Elements of Negligence
[P] To win a personal injury case in California, we must prove four things:
[LIST]
- **Duty:** The defendant owed you a duty of care.
- **Breach:** The defendant failed to meet that duty.
- **Causation:** That failure directly caused your injury.
- **Damages:** You suffered actual harm (medical bills, lost wages, pain and suffering).
