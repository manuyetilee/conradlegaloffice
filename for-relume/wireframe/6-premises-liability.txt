[HEADER] Types of Premises Cases
--------------------------------------------------
[H2] Dangerous Conditions We Litigate
[P] Premises liability covers more than just "slip and fall" accidents. We represent clients injured by:
[LIST]
- **Slip, Trip & Fall:** Wet floors, uneven sidewalks, and loose carpeting.
- **Negligent Security:** Assaults or injuries occurring due to inadequate lighting or lack of security personnel (e.g., at nightclubs or parking lots).
- **Structural Hazards:** Collapsing staircases, deck failures, and code violations.
- **Dog Bites:** Injuries caused by unrestrained or dangerous pets.

--------------------------------------------------

[HEADER] Understanding the Law
--------------------------------------------------
[H2] The Duty of Care
[P] In California, a property owner's responsibility depends on your status on the property:
[LIST]
- **Invitees:** Customers or business guests owed the highest duty of care. Owners must inspect for and fix hazards.
- **Licensees:** Social guests. Owners must warn of known dangers.
- **Trespassers:** While duties are limited, owners cannot willfully create traps or hazards.

[H2] Proving Your Case
[P] We work to prove that:
1. A hazardous condition existed.
2. The owner knew (or should have known) about it.
3. The owner failed to repair or warn you.
4. This negligence directly caused your injury.
