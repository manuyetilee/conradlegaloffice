[HEADER] Understanding Wrongful Death Claims
--------------------------------------------------
[H2] What is a Wrongful Death Claim?
[P] A wrongful death claim is a civil lawsuit filed against a person or entity whose negligence or intentional act caused a fatality. This can stem from car accidents, defective products, medical malpractice, or unsafe premises.

[H2] Who Can File in California?
[P] California law allows specific family members to seek compensation, typically including:
[LIST]
- Surviving spouses or domestic partners
- Children
- Financial dependents

[HEADER] Seeking Compensation
--------------------------------------------------
[H2] Recovering What Was Lost
[P] While no amount of money can replace a loved one, a successful claim can provide financial stability for the survivors. We fight for:
[LIST]
- **Economic Damages:** Funeral and burial expenses, lost future income, and loss of financial support.
- **Non-Economic Damages:** Loss of love, companionship, comfort, and guidance.
