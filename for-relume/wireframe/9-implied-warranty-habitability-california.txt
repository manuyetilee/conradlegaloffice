[H2] The Implied Warranty of Habitability
[P] In California, landlords are legally required to provide a property that is fit for human habitation. This includes effective waterproofing, working plumbing, heating, and freedom from pests. If these standards are not met, tenants may have the right to withhold rent, repair and deduct, or terminate the lease early without penalty.
