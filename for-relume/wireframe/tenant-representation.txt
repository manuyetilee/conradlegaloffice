### Understanding Habitability in California (H2)
Under California law, landlords must maintain rental units in a condition fit for human habitation. This "implied warranty of habitability" means landlords are responsible for making sure their properties are free from substantial defects that could harm tenants' health or safety. Common habitability issues include:
- Pest Infestations:The presence of roaches, rats, or other vermin can pose serious health risks.
- Plumbing Problems:Leaking pipes, clogged drains, or sewage backups can lead to unsanitary living conditions.
- Mold Growth:Excess moisture and poor ventilation can result in mold, which may cause respiratory issues and other health problems.
- Structural Defects:Issues like faulty wiring, broken stairs, or leaky roofs can compromise the safety of the dwelling.
These conditions not only violate housing codes but also infringe upon tenants' rights to a habitable living environment.
