### How Does California Define Wrongful Death? (H3)
In California, wrongful death is defined as a death caused by the negligence or misconduct of another person or entity. The law permits certain individuals, like spouses, children, or others who depend financially on the deceased, to file a claim. This legal action seeks compensation for their loss, including lost wages, loss of companionship, and funeral expenses. The aim is to hold the responsible party accountable and to provide financial support to the bereaved family.
